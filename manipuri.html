Manipuri – Classical Dance

Manipuri is a graceful classical dance form from the state of Manipur in Northeast India. It is known for its soft, gentle movements and devotional themes, especially those related to Lord Krishna and Radha.

Unlike other classical dances, Manipuri focuses more on smooth and flowing movements rather than strong footwork. The dancers wear beautiful traditional costumes with long skirts and delicate decorations.

The dance expresses spirituality, love, and devotion through subtle facial expressions and elegant body movements.

History

Manipuri dance has its roots in ancient religious traditions and rituals of Manipur. It became popular through the Ras Lila performances, which depict the divine love of Radha and Krishna.

Over time, it developed into a classical dance form and is now recognized as an important part of Indian culture.

Main Elements

Ras Lila: Dance drama based on Krishna and Radha.

Lasya: Soft and graceful feminine movements.

Tandava: More energetic dance style.

Costume: Traditional long skirts and decorative attire.
