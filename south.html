Famous South Indian Foods

Dosa

Dosa is a thin and crispy pancake made from fermented rice and lentil batter. It is one of the most popular South Indian dishes and is served with chutney and sambar.

Idli

Idli is a soft and fluffy steamed rice cake that is very healthy and easy to digest. It is commonly eaten for breakfast in South India with coconut chutney and sambar.

Sambar

Sambar is a spicy and tangy lentil soup made with vegetables and tamarind. It is an important part of South Indian meals and is served with idli, dosa, or rice.

Uttapam

Uttapam is a thick pancake made from fermented batter and topped with vegetables like onions, tomatoes, and chilies. It is soft and very tasty.

Medu Vada

Medu Vada is a crispy fried snack made from urad dal batter. It has a round shape with a hole in the center and is usually served with chutney and sambar.

Appe

Appe is a popular South Indian snack made from fermented rice and lentil batter.It is cooked in a special pan with small round molds and can be prepared as a savory or sweet dish.
