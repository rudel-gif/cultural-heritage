Famous West Indian Foods

Pav Bhaji

Pav Bhaji is a famous street food from Maharashtra. It consists of spicy mashed vegetables served with buttered bread rolls called pav.

Vada Pav

Vada Pav is a popular snack from Mumbai. It is made of a spicy potato fritter placed inside a bread bun and served with chutneys.

Dhokla

Dhokla is a soft and spongy snack from Gujarat made from fermented rice and gram flour. It is light, healthy, and easy to digest.

Thepla

Thepla is a traditional Gujarati flatbread made with wheat flour, spices, and herbs. It is commonly eaten for breakfast or travel food.

Misal Pav

Misal Pav is a spicy curry made from sprouted lentils and topped with crunchy farsan. It is a popular breakfast dish in Maharashtra.
