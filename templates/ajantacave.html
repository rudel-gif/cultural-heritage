About Ajanta Caves

Ajanta Caves are one of the most famous ancient rock-cut caves in India located in Maharashtra.


These caves are known for beautiful Buddhist paintings, sculptures, and ancient architecture.


The caves were built around the 2nd century BCE and are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Explore More About Ajanta Caves

Ajanta Caves are one of the greatest historical treasures of India.


Location of Ajanta Caves

Ajanta Caves are located near Aurangabad district in Maharashtra, India.


History of Ajanta Caves

The caves were built by Buddhist monks between the 2nd century BCE and 6th century CE. These caves were used for prayer, meditation, and education.


Architecture

The caves are carved into rocky hills and contain detailed sculptures, paintings, prayer halls, and monasteries.


Famous Paintings

Ajanta paintings show stories from the life of Buddha and ancient Indian culture.


Interesting Facts

  • Ajanta Caves are more than 2000 years old.
  • There are around 30 caves in Ajanta.
  • UNESCO declared it a World Heritage Site in 1983.
  • The caves attract tourists from all over the world.
