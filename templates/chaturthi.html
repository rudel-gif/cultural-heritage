Ganesh Chaturthi is a festival of Lord Ganesha

Ganesh Chaturthi is one of the most popular Hindu festivals celebrated in India, especially in Maharashtra. It marks the birth of Lord Ganesha, the god of wisdom, prosperity, and good fortune. The festival usually falls in the months of August or September and lasts for ten days. Devotees bring beautifully decorated idols of Lord Ganesha into their homes and public places to worship him.

During the festival, people offer prayers, sing devotional songs, and perform aarti every day. Special sweets called modak, which are believed to be Lord Ganesha's favorite food, are prepared and distributed among family members and visitors. Houses and streets are decorated with flowers, lights, and colorful rangoli designs to create a joyful atmosphere.

Ganesh Chaturthi also promotes unity and togetherness among people. Communities gather to celebrate the festival with cultural programs, music, and dance performances. On the final day, known as Ganesh Visarjan, the idols of Lord Ganesha are taken in a grand procession and immersed in rivers or seas, symbolizing his return to Mount Kailash.

The festival teaches people the importance of devotion, cleanliness, and harmony. It is celebrated with great enthusiasm and devotion across the country, bringing happiness and blessings to everyone who participates in the celebration.
