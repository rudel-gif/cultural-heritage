About Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is one of the most beautiful historical monuments located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. It is popularly known as the “Palace of Winds”.


The monument was built in 1799 by Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh. It is famous for its unique honeycomb design and beautiful pink sandstone architecture.


Hawa Mahal has 953 small windows called “Jharokhas” that allow cool air to pass through the palace.

Location of Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is located in Jaipur, the Pink City of Rajasthan, India. It stands in the heart of the old city.


History of Hawa Mahal

The palace was constructed so royal women could observe street festivals and daily life without being seen by the public.


Architecture

Hawa Mahal is made from pink and red sandstone. Its five-story structure resembles the crown of Lord Krishna.


Interesting Facts

  • Hawa Mahal has 953 windows.
  • It is designed in Rajput architecture style.
  • The palace remains naturally cool even during summer.
  • It is one of Jaipur’s most visited tourist attractions.
