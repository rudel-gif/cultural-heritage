About Lotus Temple

The Lotus Temple is one of the most famous landmarks in New Delhi, India. It is a Baháʼí House of Worship known for its stunning lotus-shaped architecture.


Completed in 1986, the temple welcomes people of all religions and backgrounds. It symbolizes peace, unity, and harmony among humanity.


The structure consists of 27 marble petals arranged in clusters to form a lotus flower.

Explore More About Lotus Temple

The Lotus Temple is one of the most visited religious monuments in the world.

Location

The Lotus Temple is located in New Delhi, India.

History

The temple was designed by Iranian architect Fariborz Sahba and completed in 1986.

Architecture

The building resembles a blooming lotus flower with 27 marble petals arranged in groups of three.

Main Attractions

  • Lotus-shaped Design
  • Beautiful Gardens
  • Meditation Hall
  • Reflecting Pools
  • Peaceful Environment

Interesting Facts

  • Open to people of all religions.
  • One of the most visited buildings in the world.
  • Built using white marble from Greece.
  • Can accommodate around 2,500 visitors at a time.
