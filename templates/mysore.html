About Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is one of the most famous historical monuments in India located in Mysuru, Karnataka. It is the official residence of the Wadiyar dynasty and one of the most visited palaces in India.


The palace is known for its Indo-Saracenic architecture, beautiful interiors, colorful lighting, royal halls, and grand celebrations during the Mysore Dasara Festival.


Millions of tourists visit Mysore Palace every year to experience its royal beauty, history, and cultural significance.

Explore More About Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is one of the grandest royal palaces in India and an important symbol of Karnataka’s cultural heritage.


Location of Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace is located in Mysuru city in Karnataka, India. It stands in the heart of the city and attracts visitors from around the world.


Who Built Mysore Palace?

The palace was originally built by the Wadiyar dynasty. The current structure was designed by British architect Henry Irwin and completed in 1912.


History of Mysore Palace

Mysore Palace has a rich royal history connected with the Wadiyar rulers. The earlier wooden palace was destroyed by fire in 1897, after which the present palace was constructed.


Architecture

The palace showcases Indo-Saracenic architecture with a blend of Hindu, Mughal, Rajput, and Gothic styles. It features beautiful domes, arches, stained glass ceilings, and royal courtyards.


Main Attractions Inside Mysore Palace

  • Durbar Hall – A magnificent royal hall with decorative ceilings and pillars.
  • Golden Throne – A royal throne displayed during the Dasara festival.
  • Kalyana Mantapa – A grand marriage hall with colorful stained glass.
  • Palace Illumination – The palace lights up beautifully with thousands of bulbs at night.

Famous Mysore Dasara Festival

Mysore Palace becomes the center of celebration during the Dasara Festival, featuring cultural events, music, dance, and grand processions.


Interesting Facts

  • Mysore Palace is illuminated with nearly 100,000 bulbs during festivals.
  • It is one of the most visited monuments in India after the Taj Mahal.
  • The palace has beautiful paintings, carvings, and royal artifacts.
  • The architecture reflects a mix of Indian and European styles.
