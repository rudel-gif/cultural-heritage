About Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is one of the most famous monuments in Mumbai. It was built in 1924 to welcome King George V and Queen Mary to India.


Located near the Arabian Sea, the monument attracts millions of tourists every year. It is a symbol of Mumbai’s cultural heritage and history.
