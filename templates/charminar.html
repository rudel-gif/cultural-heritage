About Charminar

Charminar is one of the most famous monuments in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. It was built in 1591 by Sultan Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah.


The monument is known for its four grand minarets and beautiful Indo-Islamic architecture. Charminar is considered the symbol of Hyderabad.


It was constructed to celebrate the end of a deadly plague and later became an important cultural landmark.

Location of Charminar

Charminar is located in the heart of Hyderabad city in Telangana, India.


History of Charminar

The monument was built in 1591 by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah, the founder of Hyderabad. It was built after the plague ended in the city.


Architecture

Charminar is built using granite and limestone. The monument has four elegant minarets, each about 56 meters high.


Interesting Facts

  • Charminar means “Four Towers”.
  • It is one of the most famous landmarks in India.
  • There is a mosque on the top floor of the monument.
  • Nearby markets are famous for pearls and bangles.
