About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the World and a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Agra, India. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal.


Made entirely of white marble, the monument is famous for its beautiful architecture, symmetrical gardens, and historical importance.


Every year, millions of tourists visit the Taj Mahal to admire its beauty and rich cultural heritage.

Explore More About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the World and the most beautiful monument in India.


Location of Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is located in Agra city in the state of Uttar Pradesh, India. It is situated on the banks of the Yamuna River and attracts millions of tourists every year.


Who Built the Taj Mahal?

The Taj Mahal was built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal. Construction started in 1632 and was completed in 1653.


History of Taj Mahal

Mumtaz Mahal died during childbirth in 1631. Shah Jahan was deeply saddened by her death and decided to build a magnificent white marble monument in her memory. The Taj Mahal became a symbol of eternal love and devotion.


Architecture

The Taj Mahal is built using pure white marble and combines Persian, Islamic, and Indian architectural styles. The monument includes beautiful domes, gardens, fountains, carvings, and calligraphy designs.


Main Attractions Inside Taj Mahal

  • Main Dome – The large white marble dome is the most iconic part of the monument.
  • Reflecting Pool – A beautiful water pool reflects the image of the Taj Mahal.
  • Mosque – A red sandstone mosque is located beside the monument.
  • Gardens – The Taj Mahal is surrounded by beautiful Mughal gardens.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

UNESCO declared the Taj Mahal a World Heritage Site in 1983 because of its historical, cultural, and architectural importance.


Interesting Facts

  • The Taj Mahal changes color during different times of the day.
  • More than 20,000 workers participated in its construction.
  • It is one of the most visited monuments in the world.
  • The Taj Mahal is known as the “Symbol of Love”.
