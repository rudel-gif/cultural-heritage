About Kashmir Folk Music

Kashmir folk music is one of the most beautiful and soulful musical traditions of India. It reflects the rich culture, natural beauty, traditions, festivals, and daily life of the people of Kashmir. Folk songs are closely connected with village life, seasonal celebrations, weddings, religious occasions, and traditional customs.


The music is famous for its melodious tunes, poetic lyrics, and emotional performances that express the feelings and experiences of Kashmiri people. Traditional folk forms such as Rouf, Chakri, and Wanwun are widely performed during festivals, weddings, and cultural gatherings.


Kashmir folk music uses traditional instruments like the santoor, rabab, tumbaknari, sarangi, and noet. The gentle melodies of the santoor and the rhythmic beats of the tumbaknari create the unique identity of Kashmiri folk music.


Folk songs of Kashmir are passed down from generation to generation and help preserve the history, traditions, and cultural heritage of the region. Popular themes in folk music include love, nature, mountains, seasons, devotion, and everyday life.


Today, Kashmir folk music is admired across India and around the world. It continues to inspire modern musicians and cultural performances while keeping the traditional heritage and artistic spirit of Kashmir alive.

Traditional Instruments

Santoor

A traditional string instrument played with wooden mallets, known for its sweet and soothing sound.

Rabab

A stringed instrument that produces deep, rich melodies in folk and devotional music.

Surnai

A double-reed wind instrument known for its loud and festive sound

Nagada

Large drums played during festivals and cultural events.
