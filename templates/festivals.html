Festivals of India

Journey Through Indian Celebrations

Discover the festivals that bring together devotion, color, music and joy.

Diwali

The festival of lights symbolizing victory of good over evil.

Holi

The vibrant celebration of colors, love and spring.

Dussehra

Celebrating the triumph of righteousness and truth.

Navratri

Nine nights of devotion, dance and divine feminine power.

Ganesh Chaturthi

Welcoming Lord Ganesha with grandeur and devotion.

Janmashtami

The divine birth celebration of Lord Krishna.

Raksha Bandhan

A sacred bond of love between brothers and sisters.

Pongal

South India's thanksgiving harvest celebration.

Makar Sankranti

A harvest festival marked with kites, sweets and sunlight.
