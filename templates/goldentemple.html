About Golden Temple

The Golden Temple, also known as Sri Harmandir Sahib, is the holiest shrine of Sikhism and one of the most visited religious places in India.


Located in Amritsar, Punjab, the temple is famous for its stunning golden architecture and the sacred Amrit Sarovar (Pool of Nectar) surrounding it.


The temple symbolizes equality, brotherhood, and selfless service, welcoming people of all faiths.

Explore More About Golden Temple

The Golden Temple is one of India's most sacred and beautiful religious monuments.

Location

The Golden Temple is located in Amritsar, Punjab, India.

History

Guru Arjan Dev Ji laid the foundation stone in 1581. The temple became the spiritual center of Sikhism.

Architecture

The temple is covered with gold-plated copper and beautiful marble work. It is surrounded by the holy Amrit Sarovar.

Main Attractions

  • Harmandir Sahib – Main shrine covered in gold.
  • Amrit Sarovar – Sacred pool around the temple.
  • Akal Takht – Seat of Sikh authority.
  • Langar Hall – World's largest free community kitchen.

Religious Importance

The Guru Granth Sahib, the holy scripture of Sikhism, is placed inside the temple and worshipped daily.

Interesting Facts

  • More than 100,000 people eat free meals daily.
  • The temple welcomes people of all religions.
  • Its four entrances symbolize openness to all.
  • The upper floors are covered with real gold.
