About Assam Folk Music

Assam folk music is one of the most beautiful and melodious musical traditions of India. It reflects the rich culture, traditions, natural beauty, and daily life of the people of Assam. Folk songs are closely connected with festivals, farming activities, village life, religious celebrations, and traditional customs.


The music is famous for its energetic rhythms, emotional lyrics, and joyful performances that bring people together during cultural events. Traditional dance forms like Bihu dance are performed with Assamese folk music during festivals and celebrations. These performances express happiness, unity, and cultural pride.


Assam folk music uses traditional instruments like the dhol, pepa, toka, gogona, and taal. The energetic beats of the dhol and the unique sound of the pepa create the special identity of Assamese folk music.


Folk songs of Assam are passed from generation to generation and preserve the history, traditions, and emotions of Assamese culture. Popular themes in folk music include nature, love, farming life, festivals, devotion, and the beauty of rivers and villages.


Today, Assam folk music is loved across India and continues to inspire modern music and cultural performances while keeping the traditional heritage of Assam alive.

Traditional Instruments

Pepa

Pepa is a traditional wind instrument made from buffalo horn and is famous for its unique energetic sound.

Gogona

Gogona is a bamboo musical instrument mainly played during Bihu festivals by Assamese women.

Taal

Taal is a pair of small cymbals used in devotional songs and traditional folk performances.

Algoza

Algoza is a pair of wooden flutes played together to produce melodious folk tunes.
