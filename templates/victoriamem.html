About Victoria Memorial

The Victoria Memorial is one of the most iconic monuments in Kolkata, West Bengal. Built in memory of Queen Victoria, it is one of India's finest examples of Indo-Saracenic architecture.


Constructed using white Makrana marble, the memorial combines British, Mughal, Venetian, and Egyptian architectural influences.


Today, it serves as a museum showcasing paintings, historical artifacts, manuscripts, and galleries related to India's colonial history.

Explore More About Victoria Memorial

Victoria Memorial is one of the most visited landmarks in Kolkata and a symbol of the city's rich historical heritage.

Location

The Victoria Memorial is located in Kolkata, West Bengal, on the famous Maidan grounds.

History

The memorial was proposed by Lord Curzon after the death of Queen Victoria in 1901. Construction began in 1906 and the monument was opened in 1921.

Architecture

The structure is built from white Makrana marble and features a grand central dome, decorative sculptures, and beautiful gardens.

Main Attractions

  • Central Dome and Angel of Victory
  • Historical Museum Galleries
  • Royal Portrait Collections
  • Beautiful Landscaped Gardens
  • Colonial Architecture

Museum Collections

The memorial contains paintings, rare books, manuscripts, sculptures, royal artifacts, and historical exhibits.

Interesting Facts

  • Built between 1906 and 1921.
  • Made from the same Makrana marble used for the Taj Mahal.
  • Features Indo-Saracenic architecture.
  • One of Kolkata's most famous tourist attractions.
