About Red Fort

The Red Fort is one of the most famous historical monuments in India located in Delhi. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1648 using beautiful red sandstone.


The fort served as the main residence of Mughal emperors for nearly 200 years. It is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a symbol of India’s rich history.


Every year on Independence Day, the Prime Minister of India hoists the national flag at Red Fort.

The Red Fort is one of the most iconic monuments of India and a masterpiece of Mughal architecture.


History of Red Fort

The Red Fort was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1648 when he shifted the capital of the Mughal Empire from Agra to Delhi. The fort was designed by architect Ustad Ahmad Lahori, who also designed the Taj Mahal.


Architecture

The fort is made using beautiful red sandstone, which gives the monument its famous red color. It combines Persian, Timurid, and Indian architectural styles. Inside the fort, there are beautiful gardens, royal halls, mosques, and palaces.


Important Places Inside the Fort

  • Diwan-i-Aam – Hall where the emperor met common people.
  • Diwan-i-Khas – Hall for royal meetings and special guests.
  • Rang Mahal – Beautiful palace for royal women.
  • Moti Masjid – A small white marble mosque inside the fort.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

UNESCO declared Red Fort as a World Heritage Site because of its historical and cultural importance. It is one of the most visited tourist attractions in India.


Independence Day Celebration

Every year on 15th August, the Prime Minister of India hoists the national flag at the Red Fort and addresses the nation from its historic walls.
