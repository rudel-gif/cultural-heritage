About Meenakshi Temple

Meenakshi Amman Temple is one of the most famous Hindu temples in India located in Madurai, Tamil Nadu.


The temple is dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi, a form of Parvati, and Lord Sundareswarar, a form of Shiva.


It is renowned for its magnificent gopurams, colorful sculptures, thousand-pillar hall, and rich Dravidian architecture.

Explore More About Meenakshi Temple

The Meenakshi Temple is one of South India's greatest architectural and spiritual landmarks.

Location

The temple is located in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, on the banks of the Vaigai River.

History

The temple dates back many centuries and was extensively rebuilt and expanded by the Nayak rulers during the 16th and 17th centuries.

Architecture

The temple complex features 14 magnificent gopurams covered with thousands of colorful sculptures of gods, goddesses, and mythological figures.

Main Attractions

  • 14 Giant Gopurams
  • Golden Lotus Pond
  • 1000 Pillar Hall
  • Meenakshi Shrine
  • Sundareswarar Shrine

Religious Importance

The temple is one of the most important pilgrimage sites for devotees of Goddess Parvati and Lord Shiva.

Interesting Facts

  • The temple contains around 985 carved pillars in the famous hall.
  • The southern tower rises over 170 feet high.
  • Thousands of sculptures decorate the temple towers.
  • The Chithirai Festival attracts millions of devotees.
