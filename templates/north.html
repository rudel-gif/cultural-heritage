Famous North Indian Foods

Butter Chicken

Butter Chicken is a popular North Indian dish made with tender chicken cooked in a creamy tomato-based gravy. It is commonly served with naan or rice.

Naan

Naan is a soft and fluffy flatbread baked in a traditional clay oven called a tandoor. It is usually eaten with curries and gravies.

Chole Bhature

Chole Bhature is a famous Punjabi dish consisting of spicy chickpea curry served with deep-fried bread called bhature.

Paneer Tikka

Paneer Tikka is a delicious vegetarian dish made by marinating paneer cubes in spices and grilling them until smoky and flavorful.
