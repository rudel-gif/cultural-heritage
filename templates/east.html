Famous East Indian Foods

Rasgulla

Rasgulla is a famous sweet from West Bengal made from soft cheese balls soaked in sugar syrup. It is one of the most popular desserts in East India.

Fish Curry

Fish Curry is a traditional dish in Bengal and Odisha. It is prepared with fresh fish cooked in a spicy gravy and served with rice.

Momos

Momos are steamed dumplings popular in the northeastern states of India. They are filled with vegetables or meat and served with spicy chutney.

Litti Chokha

Litti Chokha is a traditional dish from Bihar made of wheat balls stuffed with roasted gram flour and served with mashed vegetables.

Sandesh

Sandesh is a delicious Bengali sweet made from fresh paneer and sugar. It is often prepared during festivals and celebrations.
