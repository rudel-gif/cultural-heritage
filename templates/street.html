Famous Street Foods of India

Pani Puri

Pani Puri is one of the most popular street foods in India. It consists of crispy hollow puris filled with spicy water, potatoes, and chickpeas.

Bhel Puri

Bhel Puri is a tasty snack made from puffed rice, vegetables, and tangy chutneys. It is commonly found at street stalls across India.

Samosa

Samosa is a deep-fried snack filled with spicy potatoes and peas. It is crispy on the outside and very popular as a street food.

Aloo Tikki

Aloo Tikki is a delicious fried potato patty served with chutneys and yogurt. It is widely sold by street vendors in many cities.

Chaat

Chaat is a popular Indian street food made with a mix of crispy snacks, potatoes, yogurt, and spicy sauces. It is loved for its tangy flavor.
