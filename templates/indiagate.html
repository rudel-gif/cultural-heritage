About India Gate

India Gate is one of the most famous historical monuments located in New Delhi, India. It was built as a war memorial to honor the brave Indian soldiers who lost their lives during World War I and the Third Anglo-Afghan War.

The monument was designed by the famous British architect Sir Edwin Lutyens and was completed in the year 1931. India Gate stands proudly at a height of 42 meters and is made from beautiful sandstone.

Beneath the arch of India Gate is the Amar Jawan Jyoti, an eternal flame that honors the unknown soldiers of India. It is one of the most respected memorials in the country.

India Gate becomes especially beautiful at night when it is illuminated with lights. The surrounding gardens and fountains make it a popular tourist attraction and picnic destination for families and visitors.
