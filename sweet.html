Famous Indian Sweets

Gulab Jamun

Gulab Jamun is a soft and sweet dessert made from milk solids and soaked in sugar syrup. It is one of the most loved sweets in India.

Jalebi

Jalebi is a crispy and spiral-shaped sweet soaked in sugar syrup. It is commonly enjoyed during festivals and celebrations.

Laddu

Laddu is a traditional Indian sweet made from flour, sugar, and ghee. It is often prepared during religious festivals and special occasions.

Barfi

Barfi is a delicious milk-based sweet that comes in many varieties such as kaju barfi and coconut barfi. It is popular across India.

Rasmalai

Rasmalai is a soft and spongy dessert made from paneer balls soaked in sweetened milk flavored with cardamom and saffron.
