Mohiniyattam – Classical Dance

Mohiniyattam is a classical dance form from Kerala, known for its graceful, soft, and feminine movements. The name "Mohiniyattam" means the dance of the enchantress (Mohini), a female avatar of Lord Vishnu.

This dance form is characterized by slow, flowing movements and gentle expressions. It focuses on elegance and beauty, with emphasis on hand gestures, facial expressions, and rhythmic steps.

The dancers wear traditional white and gold costumes, and the performance is usually accompanied by classical music.

History

Mohiniyattam developed in the temples of Kerala and has roots in ancient dance traditions. It gained popularity during the 18th and 19th centuries under royal patronage.

Later, it was revived and promoted by artists and institutions to preserve its cultural importance.

Main Elements

Lasya: Graceful and feminine dance style.

Abhinaya: Expression through face and gestures.

Costume: White and gold traditional attire.

Music: Soft classical Carnatic music.
